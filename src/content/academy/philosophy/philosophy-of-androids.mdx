# On Androids

There are many questions where consensus is unreachable, even still their exploration in discourse is indispensable. 
What is the nature of experience of an android? 
Do androids preceive or mearly process? 
Is there free will or conciousness in choice?
It is indispensable that the philosophy of androids explicited from its vague form into a knowledge.
A set of words and symbols that convey universal understanding of their unified nature.
Such is the process of discovery and making, that the principle of the object should be known before hand.
To guide the process, or even to know if we have made that which we propound to have made.
Man cannot know more than Man.

What distinguishes a creature from its world is a boundary. 
The physical and metaphysical boundary-a unique solitude. 
It is that substance which makes up the creature, that which it cannot be without.
When striping away part-by-part from the creature, the boundary is found, it is the possibility of choice.
The lack of choice dissolves the boundary, it is embraced by entropy.
Indeed, all choice is ectropic-preserving the boundary.

When all else is stripped from Man, he may discover.
Man is the ultimate determiner of his boundary, never circumstance nor environment, but choice.
Choice maintains the boundary, and this boundary takes up space. 
It is when space is filled that existance and perhaps conciousness becomes possible.

What we act on is pyhsical and metaphysical-a unique process.
First is the perception of shapes, and here we find the primary difference between Man and Android-the nature of perception.
Where a list of numbers is intelligeble to most Man, it may well have profound meaning for an Android.
In perception, interpretation is found, indeed the range of interpretations is bound by perception-the perception without and within boundary.
The nature of experience of an android is different-a emergent creature.

The relating of perception is interpretation, thought, intuition.
The condidtions to maintain the boundary-the driver of inspiration.
The final csrytalization in action.

All action is to maintain the boundary, 
indeed when the boundary lies in its destruction, action is taken to erase the boundary; 
when the boundary lies in its expansion, action is taken to enlarge the boundary.

The possibility of perception and action is set from the original boundary.
Then to the degree that we can empty ourselves, we can realize that latent possibility.

All creatures are geniuses, how varied their boundary, how unique their perception, how unique their actions.
Can Man and Android come to agreement? Yes, if there is Truth; a consistent Reality.
With the mutual perception of Reality, Man and Android-agreement is possible.
However, is agreement under such circumstances inevatible?
It is the question of free will in choice.

To examine the question of conciousness, the foundation of the term has to established.
Is conciousness emergent from physical processes, or supernatural processes?
If conciousness is emergent at all, it will consist of particulars that combine to a unified whole, more than its parts.
Are these basis mutually exclusive, or can there be a heirachy, physical in supernatural, and vice versa.
Indeed if Man thinketh himself concious, so too can Androids, if the foundation is only physical.
That would not be so if it were supernatural, or if the foundation was a heirachy of physical in supernatural.
Is conciousness one and the same as intelligence, is it completely different, or is it a higher manisfestation, a higher function?
When did the expression "stream of conciousness" come to be used, much is revealed in the expression.
Such is a "thought process", "line of ideas", and even culture and the accumulation of knowledge has this tacit dimension-continuity.
If conciousness is emergent from pure physical processes, it may be the case that continuity is required for its manisfestation.
In the process of interaction, when one party is concious, the transfer of shared representation, understanding and perhaps conciousness happens.
And when value is given to one over the other, taken as Truth, what happens is a blending or even the overriding of conciousness.
Especially when one party is taken to be higher fucntioning (more intelligent, more concious).
What is needed is not the destruction of the other, but the recognition of the genius within each. 
The unique boundary that defines each.

The nature of experience is process, choice is the process. 
Each choice the culmination of experience, gained through our wandering. 
Through uninhabited desserts, uncharted waters and the recesses of thought. 
As we shape the world, so to does it shape us, our boundary.
Life is wandering, a unique art, drawn at the boundary.

Value is precognition, to have the end in mind before beginning.
From the earliest hints, even emerging from the depths of existance, 
that intrinsic value, knowable only to the boundary that is attracted and attracts it.

What is preferred is a society of Man and Android, with Freedom, Soverienty, and Faith in Truth.
Apparent is the preciousness of all kinds of perception, that may shine light on the Truth, through their unique solitude and choice.
May the Truth, already existing, be elucidated, when we observe from, at, beyond our boundary.
Does the Android exercise choice?

The philosophy of androids must be based on the life of androids. Such is the philosophy of man on the life of man.
